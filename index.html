RESEARCH PERSPECTIVE

My research spans the journey of air pollution: how particles form, how pollutants move and react, and how observations and models can explain their environmental impacts.

01 / RESEARCH

Three connected research directions.

Experimental foundations.
Atmospheric questions.
Data-informed understanding.

01

Emissions &
aerosol science

Combustion-generated particulate matter, soot morphology and nanostructure, alternative fuels, and the influence of engine operating conditions on particle properties.

  • Soot characterisation
  • Particle imaging
  • Alternative fuels
02

Wildfire smoke &
atmospheric chemistry

Transported wildfire emissions, particulate matter and urban ozone. Integrating atmospheric observations and modelling to investigate pollutant behaviour and ozone formation.

  • Wildfire emissions
  • PM₂.₅ & ozone
  • Model–observation integration
03

Environmental
data science

Machine learning and meteorological normalisation to separate weather and human influences on air quality, with emphasis on physical interpretation and robust validation.

  • Machine learning
  • Attribution
  • Reproducible analysis

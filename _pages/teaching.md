## Teaching

At QUT, I supported tutorials, laboratory activities and assessment during my doctoral research, using experimental and environmental engineering examples to connect theory with measurement and interpretation.

At MANIT Bhopal, I contributed postgraduate teaching and workshops in air quality and practical data analysis. My invited teaching covers machine learning, meteorological normalisation, emissions technology, soot characterisation, biofuels and clean energy.

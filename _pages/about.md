I am an Adjunct Senior Lecturer in the School of Population and Global Health at the **University of Western Australia**. My research brings together mechanical and environmental engineering, aerosol science, atmospheric chemistry, and environmental data science.

I study how emissions form, how pollutants move and react in the atmosphere, and how measurements and models can help us understand air pollution. My experimental work focuses on combustion-generated particles, particularly the morphology and nanostructure of soot. My collaborative research also examines wildfire smoke, particulate matter, ozone formation, and the use of machine learning to distinguish meteorological and human influences on air quality.

I completed my PhD at **Queensland University of Technology** in 2019, awarded in 2020, investigating the effects of oxygenated fuels on diesel soot. Previously, I completed an M.Tech in Energy Systems at **IIT Roorkee** and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at **Punjab Technical University**.

## Research interests

* Combustion emissions, aerosol properties, soot morphology and nanostructure.
* Wildfire smoke, atmospheric transport, particulate matter and ozone chemistry.
* Environmental observations, machine learning and meteorological normalisation.
* Alternative fuels and their environmental impacts.

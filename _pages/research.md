## Emissions and aerosol science

My experimental research examines combustion-generated particulate matter, with a focus on soot morphology and nanostructure. This work investigates how fuel composition, oxygenated additives and engine operating conditions influence the properties of emitted particles. It includes particle imaging, image analysis and comparisons between cold-start, hot-start and steady-state operation.

## Wildfire smoke and atmospheric chemistry

My collaborative research investigates transported wildfire emissions and their contributions to urban particulate matter and ozone. Related studies use long-term atmospheric observations and machine learning to examine ozone formation sensitivity and the influence of wildfire smoke on air quality in Australia.

## Environmental observations and data science

I use machine learning and meteorological normalisation to investigate the roles of weather and human activity in observed air pollution. My interests include physically interpretable models, robust spatiotemporal validation, feature attribution and reproducible analysis workflows.

## Methods

* Experimental particle characterisation and image analysis.
* Atmospheric observations and model–observation comparison.
* Random forests, gradient boosting and neural networks.
* Python, R, GIS and environmental data visualisation.
